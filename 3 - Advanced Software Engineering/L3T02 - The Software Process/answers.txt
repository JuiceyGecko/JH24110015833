Question 2: Why Incremental Development is Effective for Business Software Systems

Flexibility: Incremental development adapts to changing needs without overhauls.

Quick Value: Core features are delivered early for immediate use.

User Input: Frequent releases ensure the product meets real needs.

Lower Risk: Small updates catch issues early.

Cost Control: Spread costs and adjust priorities as needed.

Market Edge: Launch essential features fast, add improvements later.

Simpler Testing: Smaller updates are easier to test thoroughly.

Question 3: Two Requirements Engineering Steps in Reuse-Oriented Process

Ideal Requirements: The first step defines what the system should do without considering existing components.

Adjustments: The second step revises these requirements based on available components.

Reasons for Two Steps:

Components may not match original needs perfectly.

Adjusting requirements is cheaper than custom development.

Focuses on what's realistic and implementable.

Highlights where new development is truly needed.

Outcome: Balances ideal goals with practical solutions, ensuring a feasible and cost-effective system.
