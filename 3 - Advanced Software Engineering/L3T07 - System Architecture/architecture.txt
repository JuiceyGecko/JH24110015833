Architecture Patterns: Examples & Combinations

Real-World Examples:

--Layered (n-tier) – E-commerce (Amazon)--

*Clear separation (UI, logic, data)

*Easy maintenance & scaling

--Microkernel – Web browsers (Chrome), IDEs (VS Code)--

*Stable core + extensible plugins

*Third-party customization

--Event-Driven – Stock trading, Uber--

*Real-time event processing

*Loose coupling & scalability

--Microservices – Netflix, Uber--

*Independent scaling & deployment

*Fault isolation & team autonomy

--Space-Based – Online gaming, ticket booking--

*In-memory data for high scalability

*Handles unpredictable traffic spikes

Combined Patterns

1.Microservices + Event-Driven (Amazon)

*Modular services + real-time event communication

2.Layered + Microkernel (WordPress)

*Clean structure (layers) + plugin extensibility
