"""1. Defining the following terms"""

Data: information that is important, such as facts or figures
that has not been organized. It is the basic building block of information.
Data can be numbers or text.

Field: is an important piece of information on one singular topic. It is the smallest
and most meaningful unit in a database. In most customer databases, the first name
is the most important part. That would be considered a field.

Record: is a complete set of related fields about one group. For customers, this would
include their phone numbers or addresses all grouped together in one "record".

File: is a collection of the necessary records. Possibly a table for all the customers'
records.

"""2. What is DBMS and what are the advantages"""

DBMS: helps create, manage and control access to databases. Some
of the advantages are improved data integration. This provides a unified view of an
organization's data. Increased productivity; there's quicker access to information.
Improved decision making; the information that the DBMS helps create will have much higher
quality information, so will help in decision making. There are quicker responses to queries.
There will be fewer errors as it eliminates multiple entries of data. Also many users are
able to gain access at the same time.

"""3. The difference between data and information"""

Information is data that has been processed and data is the unprocessed parts of numbers or text
that haven't been organized.

Data: Joseph, South Africa, 40, retired, Single

Information: Joseph is 40 years old, retired and living in South Africa

"""4. What is metadata?"""

This gathers information about data that already exists. It will give users
information from current databases.

This could be the data types, or relationships between data.
